Thermal-fluid & energy science

Teaching & Mentoring

I believe engineering education should connect fundamental theory to real systems through interactive, inclusive, and application-driven learning. My goal is a classroom where students master core concepts while developing the confidence, curiosity, and collaborative mindset to solve complex energy, thermal-fluid, and mechanical engineering problems.

Teaching Experience

  • Spring 2023

    Teaching Assistant — ME 366 “Power Generation Technologies”

    Department of Mechanical Engineering and Mechanics, Lehigh University

    Assisted with course logistics and evaluation for site visits, projects, homework, and presentations; led grading and provided written feedback. Organized a site visit to the Lehigh Energy Research Center solar concentrator, connecting classroom concepts with real power and fuel production.

Courses I Can Teach

  • Undergraduate / graduate core: thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, thermal systems, and laboratory / experimental methods.
  • Proposed elective: Data-Driven Methods for Thermal-Fluid and Energy Systems — feature extraction from experimental signals, surrogate modeling, reduced-order modeling, uncertainty-aware prediction, and physics-informed machine learning.
  • Energy-focused topics: phase-change heat transfer, thermal energy storage, energy systems, and experimental diagnostics.
